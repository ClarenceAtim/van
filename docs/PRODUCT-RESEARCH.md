# Product research – 60 new products (November 2026)

Ten new products were added to each of the six shop categories: 4 proven best sellers, 4 trending or rising products and 2 long-tail products per category. Every product was checked against the existing catalog first so there are no duplicates or renamed copies.

## Method

- **Best-seller signals:** Amazon US sales-ranking estimates (ASINSIGHT), retailer "best of" lists and long-run species popularity studies.
- **Trend signals:** a peer-reviewed Google Trends and owner-survey study of pet reptile popularity, plus hobbyist and retailer coverage of rising topics (bioactive cleanup crews, radiant heat panels, UV index meters, Lilly White crested geckos).
- **Long-tail picks:** specific product phrases with narrower intent and fewer competing pages (e.g. "crested gecko feeding ledge", "reptile heat tape").
- **Keywords:** the main keyword appears in each product's name, slug, SEO title, H1 and first sentence. Search volumes were **not** measured; no keyword-volume tool was available, so keyword choice is based on how retailers, forums and "people also ask" results phrase each product. Check volumes in Google Search Console after launch.

**Caveat:** several of the sources below are retailer or affiliate pages, and the ASINSIGHT figures are third-party Amazon estimates. They indicate demand direction, not exact sales.
