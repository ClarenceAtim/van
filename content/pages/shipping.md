## How shipping cost works

Shipping depends on where you live and what you order, so we confirm it personally. After you place your order, we email you within 24 hours with the **shipping cost, the final total and the payment details** for the method you chose. Nothing is charged until you've seen the full price.

## Reptile supplies

We ship supplies **worldwide**. Orders usually ship within 1–2 business days of payment, and we email you a tracking number as soon as they do. Frozen feeders ship Monday–Wednesday on dry ice and only within the United States.

## Live animals

- Live animals ship **overnight**, **Monday to Wednesday** only, so they never wait in a depot over a weekend.
- You choose a preferred weekday delivery date at checkout. We confirm the ship date by email.
- One overnight box covers the animals and any supplies in the same order.
- We may delay shipping in extreme heat or cold to keep your animal safe, and we'll contact you to reschedule.
- An adult (18+) must be available to receive the package.

## Where live animals can ship

Live animals currently ship within the **United States only**, and not to Alaska or Hawaii. Some states and cities restrict certain species, so please check your local laws before buying. If your address isn't on our list, checkout will tell you, and you can still order supplies.

## Customs and import duties

Orders shipped outside the United States may be charged import duties or taxes by your country. These are paid by the recipient and aren't included in our shipping cost.

## Tracking

As soon as your order ships we'll email a tracking number. You can also see it in [your account](/account/orders).
