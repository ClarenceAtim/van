## Reptile supplies

| Method | Cost | Delivery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Standard | $9.99 | 3–5 business days |
| Standard on orders over $99 | Free | 3–5 business days |

Supply orders placed before 1pm Central on a business day usually ship the same day. Frozen feeders ship Monday–Wednesday on dry ice.

## Live animals

| Method | Cost | Delivery |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Overnight (priority) | $49.99 per order | Next day by 10:30am in most areas |

- Live animals ship **Monday to Wednesday** only.
- One overnight box covers the animals and any supplies in the same order.
- We hold shipments when weather along the route is too hot or too cold, and contact you to reschedule.
- An adult (18+) must be available to receive the package.

## Where we ship

We currently ship within the **United States**. Live animals can only ship to states where the species is legal to own and where overnight delivery is reliable — you'll confirm your state at checkout. Some states and cities restrict certain species, so please check your local laws before buying.

## Tracking

As soon as your order ships we'll email a tracking number. You can also see it in [your account](/account/orders).
