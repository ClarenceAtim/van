Every reptile we ship is covered by our **Live Arrival Guarantee**: your animal will arrive alive and in the same healthy condition it left us.

## What's covered

If your animal arrives deceased or seriously unwell, we will **replace it with a comparable animal or refund the animal's price in full**, including the overnight shipping cost.

## What you need to do

1. **Be available on delivery day.** Someone 18 or older must be present to receive the package. Please don't select "leave at door".
2. **Open the box within 2 hours of delivery**, ideally while recording a short video.
3. **If there's a problem, email us within 2 hours of delivery** with photos or video of the animal still in its packaging.

## What isn't covered

- Packages refused, delayed or left undelivered because of an incorrect address or because no one was available to receive them.
- Packages held at a carrier facility at the customer's request and not collected the same day.
- Health problems that develop after the first 72 hours. Reptiles can hide illness, so we recommend a vet check within the first two weeks.

## How we keep animals safe in transit

- Animals ship **overnight only, Monday to Wednesday**, so they never sit in a depot over a weekend.
- We check the forecast along the route. If temperatures are unsafe, we hold your order and contact you to reschedule.
- Every box is insulated with heat or cold packs chosen for the forecast, and each animal travels in its own secured, ventilated container.
