_Last updated: November 1, 2026_

This policy explains what personal information we collect when you use our website and how we use it.

## Information we collect

- **Account details:** your name, email address and (optionally) phone number when you create an account.
- **Order details:** shipping address, items purchased and order history.
- **Payment details:** card payments are processed by **Stripe**. We never see or store your full card number.
- **Messages:** anything you send through our contact form.
- **Technical data:** basic server logs (IP address, browser type) used for security and to prevent abuse.

## How we use it

- To process and deliver your orders and send order and shipping emails.
- To answer your questions.
- To send our newsletter, only if you subscribe.
- To protect the site from fraud, spam and abuse (for example, rate limiting and Cloudflare Turnstile bot checks).

We **do not sell** your personal information.

## Service providers

We share the minimum information needed with: Stripe (payments), Supabase (database and accounts), Resend (email delivery), Cloudflare (bot protection), Vercel (hosting) and shipping carriers (delivery).

## Cookies

We use essential cookies only: one to keep you signed in and one to remember your cart. We don't use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Your choices

You can view and update your details in your account, or contact us to request a copy or deletion of your data. We keep order records as long as tax law requires.
