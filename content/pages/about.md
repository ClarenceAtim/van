ScaleHaven started as a spare-room gecko project and grew into a dedicated breeding facility. Today we hatch and raise every animal we sell, and we only stock equipment we use on our own collection.

## What we believe

- **Captive bred, always.** We never sell wild-caught animals. Captive-bred reptiles are healthier, parasite-free, already eating in captivity and far better adjusted to life with people.
- **Husbandry first.** A healthy reptile starts with the right enclosure, heat gradient and UVB. If something we sell isn't right for the animal you're buying, we'll tell you.
- **Honest listings.** Every animal is listed with its real care needs, adult size and lifespan, so you know exactly what you're committing to.

## How we raise our animals

Every hatchling is set up individually, started on an appropriate diet and must take **at least five consecutive meals** before it can be listed. Each animal gets a visual health check before it is packed: clear eyes, clean vent, complete sheds and good body weight.

## Who we are

We're a small team of keepers who have spent years working with ball pythons, colubrids, geckos and chelonians. We answer every email ourselves — if you have a question before or after you buy, [contact us](/contact).
