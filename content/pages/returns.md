## Supplies

You can return **unopened, unused supplies within 30 days** of delivery for a full refund of the item price.

- Contact us first so we can send return instructions.
- Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item arrived damaged or we sent the wrong item.
- Opened substrate, food, supplements and live feeder insects can't be returned for hygiene reasons.
- Damaged on arrival? Email photos within 48 hours and we'll replace it at no cost.

## Live animals

For the animal's welfare, **live animals can't be returned** once delivered. Arrival problems are covered by our [Live Arrival Guarantee](/live-arrival-guarantee).

## Refunds

Approved refunds go back to your original payment method within 5–10 business days of approval.
